# Directed speech during playback (#40)

VoiceMode keeps ordinary bounded capture enabled while replies and Announcements play. Echo cancellation and playback-reference submission retain their existing adapter path. Complete endpoint transcripts go to the same contextual assessor used for thinking continuations and Follow-up windows. Classification runs outside audio callbacks. No VAD-triggered pause, keyword-only cancellation, longer-speech rule or later overheard-stop bypass has playback authority.

The default remains `JARVIS_ADMISSION_BACKEND=off`. Disabled, unavailable, uncertain, malformed or stale assessment leaves the current output running and admits no conversational request. Opting into the subscription classifier permits bounded transcripts/context, including background speech, to leave the Mac and consumes classifier usage even when speech is rejected. Raw audio remains local. See [the provider contract](cloud-spoken-admission.md); enabling a provider does not establish its semantic accuracy.

A current floor decision selects only original whole raw segments. A correction during a request's reply retains that request's identity and creates a revision; the Hub holds existing Task authority until the Talker resolves scope. A voice-only stop creates no user turn or Task cancellation. It opens the existing bounded contextual Follow-up window, using the prior request and played prefix as context; later background speech cannot enter through raw listening or extend that expiry. Hub interruption owns obsolete generation/audio stop and drain, once. Typed replacement, source-owner changes and shutdown fence late results. Idle wake remains a separate local activation capability.

Announcements use a bounded admission window with an empty admitted request rather than a fabricated SpokenTurn. A directed new request receives its own identity; unrelated speech and uncertain decisions leave the Announcement running. Played context is empty until this Announcement's first output has begun, preventing a previous reply's prefix from being reused. The same window can retain an eligible candidate across natural Announcement completion; its source phase stays distinct from a later Follow-up onset. Quiet-mode queuing and existing news delivery remain in the Hub. Full long-candidate/news/late-completion races remain the separate #41 integration gate.

## Bounds and diagnostics

The existing endpoint, max-turn, onset-padding, capture backlog and retention bounds remain. Candidates are limited to four raw segments and the existing request/context size caps. A limit emits an on-screen notice instead of extracting a shortened request or disrupting current playback. The former `JARVIS_BARGEIN_MIN_SPEECH_MS`, `JARVIS_BARGEIN_DECIDE_MS` and `JARVIS_BARGEIN_GUARD_MS` are no longer parsed or exposed. Internal legacy partial-replay structures remain available for archived tests/evidence, but VoiceMode disables their producer and ignores queued legacy BargeIn, Partial and Overheard events. Non-idle Wake hits cannot bypass assessment. Keyword scoring keeps its existing idle-delivery behavior, but hits during ordinary capture do not become STT wake-cut metadata. A late VAD onset therefore cannot let an irrelevant keyword hit trim the candidate opening.

Existing `spoken_relation` and window-admission metrics retain candidate identity, selected indices, decision reason and classifier duration without new transcript fields. A `playback_admission` row records source phase, floor/stop decision and the main-thread stop-request time. `onset_to_stop_request_ms` is available only when a source onset exists, using the shared monotonic clock. It measures control scheduling, not physical onset-to-silence. `played_basis=queue_estimate` identifies the output progress estimate. Device buffering, fading and word alignment remain unmeasured. `stats --bargein` reads historical pause-first rows; current decisions do not emit new legacy Barge-in rows.

## Verification limits

Injected Hub/player cases cover EN/IT exact corrections, legacy/wake bypass rejection, Backchannels, background dialogue, failed/uncertain/default-off decisions, continued output during assessment, a voice-only stop preserving a background Task, typed replacement and basic Announcement admission. A Capture frame fixture retains a four-second candidate beyond pre-roll while reference submission remains active. These checks open no devices and make no real model calls.

Independent semantic quality, false pauses/stops, missed directed requests, physical onset-to-stop time, speaker echo isolation and the final integrated configuration remain unmeasured. No numeric maximum directed-stop delay was agreed. Report accuracy and delay together during the human checks; the fake scheduling tests do not close #40 or Spec #26.
