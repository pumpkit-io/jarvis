# Opt-in spoken admission

`JARVIS_ADMISSION_BACKEND=off` is the default. Setting it explicitly to `claude` permits candidate transcripts, including possible background speech, and bounded request/reply context to leave the Mac through the existing first-party Claude subscription. `JARVIS_ADMISSION_MODEL` selects the classifier model explicitly; it defaults to `sonnet`. No setting or environment file is changed automatically. Text-only mode does not construct or check this provider. [ADR 0017](../adr/0017-opt-in-cloud-spoken-admission.md) records the User's privacy decision.

This path handles resumed speech whose logical onset was during thinking or playback, including delayed transcription delivered after the reply changes phase. Full endpoint candidates use the same bounded classifier; VAD, a Wake hit or partial interrupt words cannot pause playback. Announcements use that same admission path with no invented user request owner. It preserves the already-admitted opening and selects only whole original fragments labelled floor. Ignored or uncertain background cannot ride into the Talker merely because a later fragment addresses Jarvis. Uncertain fragments can be reassessed with later observed speech; confirmed ignore fragments are discarded. On accepted floor or stop, unselected old-owner fragments are discarded. The Talker resolves Task scope under the existing durable hold; classifier relation labels cannot choose Tasks or replace raw text. A voice-only stop admits no text and does not cancel Tasks.

The observer prompt lives in `prompts/admission.md`. It retains the public synthetic cloud screen's semantic policy; only the research-only authority sentence changes to the production ownership contract. Input contains only admitted_turn, current played_text, frozen onset phase/started_before_reply and observed_segments. IDs, oracle labels, private log paths and future speech are excluded. The full JSON context is limited to 6,000 characters, with one to four nonempty original segments and a 2,048-byte result limit. Overflow abstains without truncating the opening. The ordered output must contain exactly one of floor/stop/ignore/uncertain per original segment; an invalid vector abstains atomically.

The provider starts only after opt-in. Startup checks the existing first-party subscription route without a conversational warm-up or model call. Each candidate uses a fresh, empty temporary cwd, no tools/skills/MCP/settings, deny-all tool hooks, one turn, bounded output and no retries. A prepared worker owns a private process group. The installed SDK's CLI child inherits that group; timeout/cancellation kills the owned group and waits for its disappearance before replacement. Decision timeout is at most two seconds for the whole candidate, including framing/IPC. Cleanup runs separately and prevents overlapping queries. A stale owner result has no admission authority; normal staleness does not permanently disable later candidates.

Startup/status notices distinguish disabled, loading, ready, unavailable, missing subscription and usage-limited admission. Ready means the worker and subscription check passed; it is not a model accuracy or physical readiness claim. Provider failure, invalid output or a genuine deadline disables further requests until an explicit restart; there is no silent switch or retry storm. A usage-limited classifier does not change unrelated Task or conversational recovery state. Unknown reset times are not invented. Existing replies keep playing when the classifier abstains or is unavailable.

Fake Hub/audio/provider tests cover EN/IT source selection, four-part continuation, invalid vectors, no hidden calls in disabled mode, stale ownership, quotas/deadlines and silent text-mode configuration. A local fake CLI exercises the actual installed SDK transport to prove that its child remains in the owned group and is removed after a deadline; it never contacts a model. The prior spent synthetic cloud screen completed 22/24 cases, with two unknown failures and two uncertain-to-ignore errors. No heldout input was read or model tuned for this implementation. Independent semantics, real provider readiness/deadlines, engine coexistence and physical conversation remain separate gates; fake checks cannot close #38 or #26.

Typed input preserves an initial, unadmitted recording even if its queued SpeechStarted has not reached VoiceMode yet. In the reproduced capture test, STT was already gated while VoiceMode still reported listening with no owner and in_turn false; retiring that epoch lost the eventual complete request. The no-owner capture window now survives that delivery race. A later typed request still retires an already-owned spoken generation and rejects its late assessment. These are source-ownership rules, not a longer timing threshold.


## Follow-up windows

After a reply finishes, the same opt-in assessor decides whether speech inside the existing follow-up window addresses Jarvis. With admission disabled or unavailable, follow-up speech is ignored; the explicit wake-and-request listening path remains available after the window closes. A wake name spoken inside follow-up is candidate speech, not an independent admission signal. The window keeps its original expiry when speech is ignored, uncertain, incomplete, or fails assessment. Voice-only stop closes that window without cancelling Tasks.

Eligibility uses the captured speech onset, not transcription delivery time. Speech beginning within the original window can finish transcription and assessment after expiry; an onset after expiry cannot join that candidate. Pending eligible speech also defers queued news, without extending that onset window. Late speech captured during playback cannot bypass playback admission by arriving in follow-up; the existing thinking-origin assessment remains separate. A bounded uncertain fragment may be reassessed with later eligible speech. Known ignored fragments are dropped. Overflow abandons the candidate rather than admitting a partial request. Capture failure invalidates pending local fragments, and typed input or a fresh owner rejects old results.

An ordinary reply retains its admitted SpokenTurn. Selected follow-up speech appends exact original fragments as a new revision and enters the existing durable scope hold before the Talker runs. The Talker decides whether to amend prior Tasks, preserve them, or open a separate request. An Announcement has no invented user request: its follow-up uses an empty admitted turn and a frozen estimate of the announcement text actually played, and selected speech starts a fresh spoken request.

Fake Hub tests cover EN/IT directed and background speech, unchanged expiry, delayed onset/transcription, stale typed ownership, fragment selection, capture failure, Announcement context and prior-Task authority while scope resolution waits. They establish mechanical admission and ownership behavior. Independent semantic evaluation, real provider timing and physical conversation gates remain open for #39 and #26; this change does not qualify a classifier or prove natural follow-up recognition.


Speech that began during thinking retains its original owner and fragments through natural follow-up expiry until transcription and assessment finish or capture aborts. Quiet news and idle keyword detection wait for that pending source to settle; new onsets after the original window still cannot join it. Typed replacement and explicit new authority retire it normally. Ignored, uncertain, aborted or over-limit terminal input releases pending capture and news after expiry. A superseded assessment followed only by an empty fragment also settles quiet notification without admitting stale speech or inventing another request.


Rejected source identities remain rejected through an admitted revision in the same capture epoch, including late transcription and abort events. Up to 64 identities are retained without eviction. Exceeding that bound, or receiving an untrackable rejected source, preserves known eligible pending speech but blocks unknown sources until a fresh wake creates new ownership, with one on-screen recovery notice. This prevents an expired-window fragment from becoming a new thinking continuation merely because an earlier eligible fragment was admitted first.
