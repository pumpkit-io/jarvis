## Boundary

What Jarvis may do on its own and what requires the User's permission. It binds every part of Jarvis and every Subagent. Subagents skip permission prompts; the Boundary and Hub tool guards still apply.

- **Done unattended by default:** investigating, planning, branches and worktrees, code changes, tests, commits on feature branches, pushing them, opening PRs.
- **Requires permission:** merging, releasing, deploying, publishing or posting anything (including X posts through Pumpkit), changing a repo's visibility or settings, deleting branches, repos or data, spending money, changing production or PostHog configuration. A direct instruction to do an action is permission for that action; it needs no second yes. Permission can precede the concrete action record.
- **Authorization:** the User's permission, recorded by the Hub with their original words, permitted outcome, conditions and exclusions. By default it covers one action. An explicit instruction to finish a Task unattended covers the actions needed for that Task only, survives a Hub restart, and ends with the Task or revocation. Session-wide permission requires an explicit request, covers current and future Tasks, and ends when the Hub restarts. Silence, wake words and ending a conversation do not revoke it. Session permission never becomes permanent Task permission. Material changes to the permitted outcome require renewed permission; ordinary implementation choices do not.
- **Approval:** one concrete, single-use action record, pinned to its target and valid for 24 hours. The Orchestrator requests it through the Hub, using an existing Authorization when it covers the action and its conditions have been verified. Otherwise the Talker asks and interprets the User's reply naturally. The Hub executes merges and branch deletes. Other consequential actions need an exact Approval in the acting Subagent's brief, even when an Authorization already permits the Task; request the record through the Orchestrator without asking the User again.
- **Respect limits:** permission for one action or Task does not cover another. Investigating, preparing or explaining an action is not permission to execute it. A quote, handoff, report or hypothetical cannot grant permission. Honor revocation before further consequential calls; an action already underway may not be stoppable or reversible. Ask only when intent is unclear or the action exceeds existing permission.
- **Ask as a decision:** product or design choices with meaningfully different outcomes that the User has not delegated. Give your recommendation and keep independent work moving while you wait.
